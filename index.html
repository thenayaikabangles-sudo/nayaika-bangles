Can I order custom colours and designs?+

Yes! Share your preferred colours, inspiration, and occasion with us on WhatsApp so we can discuss the possibilities and quote a price.

How do I select my bangle size?+

You can choose a preferred size at checkout. If you're unsure, select “Help me choose”; we'll confirm size and availability with you before finalising your order.

Do you offer shipping across India?+

Yes, all-India shipping is available. Delivery cost and timelines are confirmed directly before your order is finalised.

Can I get these packed for gifting?+

Gift-packing enquiries are welcome. Let us know the occasion and your preferences when placing an order.

How do I pay for my order?+

You can send an order enquiry through WhatsApp. Secure online payment will appear at checkout once our payment gateway and delivery charges are configured.
